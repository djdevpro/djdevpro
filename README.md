### 🎯 What I build

I make **documents and data readable by LLMs** — parsing, chunking, embedding and
compressing real-world content into clean, privacy-conscious, local-first tools.
My projects share one backbone: the **DocLang** document model.

---

### 🚀 Featured projects

**[doclith](https://github.com/djdevpro/doclith)** — the foundation 🧱
> TypeScript/JS implementation of **DocLang v0.6**: lossless XML parsing, typed AST,
> serialization, validation (XSD- + Schematron-equivalent), a CLI, and converters
> **Markdown / HTML / PDF → DocLang**.

**[RagViewer](https://github.com/djdevpro/RagViewer)** — RAG you can *see* 🔍
> In-browser **RAG over your PDFs**: convert to DocLang, chunk, embed, explore the
> embedding cloud, and chat with your documents. Local-first, with optional
> **Ollama · OpenAI · Qdrant**.

**[net-digest](https://github.com/djdevpro/net-digest)** — network traffic for LLMs 📡
> Turns DevTools network traffic into a compact, **secret-free TOON digest** your LLM
> can actually read — **7× smaller than a HAR**, with recorded user flow and real initiators.
